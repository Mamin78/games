How to play

  • +→

    Drag a tile onto a matching neighbor above, below, left or right. They merge into their sum on the tile you drop onto.

  • Gaps stay empty. Nothing slides into them, so tiles separated by a gap can’t merge and the order of your merges matters.

  • →

    Wildcards merge with any number. ×2 doubles it and +3 adds 3. Two wildcards can’t merge with each other.

  • Finish with a single tile. One careless merge can strand a tile, so look ahead.

Hint shows a safe next merge. If the board can no longer be solved, it rewinds you to your last good move. You can also tap a tile and then its neighbor, or use the arrow keys with Enter. Shortcuts: U undoes, H asks for a hint.
